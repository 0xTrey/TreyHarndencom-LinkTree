What I'm
doing now.

Updated September 17, 2026 Milwaukee, Wisconsin

I'm trying to turn useful AI into real operating systems, keep shipping small things, and leave enough space for a life outside the screen.

Building practical AI systems

Most of my work is focused on go-to-market systems that research accounts, turn context into useful buyer experiences, and make the work easier to inspect and repeat. The goal is not more AI theater. It is better work that actually runs.

Learning by making

I'm still a software newb. I use AI to build tools, agents, websites, and small experiments, then document what worked, what broke, and what I would change next time.

Training consistently

Running, walking, hiking, lifting, and staying alcohol-free are part of the operating system. I care more about the long trend than a perfect week.

Getting outside

Milwaukee is home base. Mountains, public parks, spicy food, and time with people I care about are the counterweight to all the screen time.

This page changes when my priorities do. The idea comes from Derek Sivers' /now movement.
